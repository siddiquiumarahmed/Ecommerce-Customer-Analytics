Project background

About the Dataset & Project

Every number in this dashboard is simulated for education. The structure and behaviour of the data, however, are modelled on two of the most famous public retail datasets used in Big Data research.

Online Retail Dataset

UCI ML Repository

A transactional dataset from a UK-based online gift retailer, covering invoices between 2010 and 2011. It's a staple for teaching customer segmentation and RFM analysis.

  • ~541,000 transactionseach row is a product line on an invoice.
  • 38 countrieswhich inspired this project's geography module.
  • 8 columnsInvoiceNo, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, UnitPrice, CustomerID, Country.

Instacart Dataset

Kaggle

Over 3 million grocery orders from 200,000 anonymised Instacart users. It's the classic dataset for Market Basket Analysis and reorder prediction.

  • 3.4M orders · 50k productsrich enough to mine reliable association rules.
  • Reorder behaviourwhich shaped this project's weekly-shopping-frequency patterns.
  • Aisle & department graphthe inspiration for the product relationship network view.

Project objectives

Goals
  • Visualise customer behavioursegmentation, retention, frequency and geography.
  • Identify best-selling productsby units, revenue, category and growth.
  • Demonstrate Market Basket Analysiswith support, confidence and lift explained simply.
  • Convert data into decisionsstock, bundles, retention and marketing actions.
  • Prove frontend crafta premium dashboard with zero frameworks or chart libraries.

What the platform delivers

Capabilities
  • Customer Analyticssegments, retention, frequency, geography and activity trends.
  • Product Intelligencerankings, category performance, monthly sales and trending items.
  • Market Basket Analysisassociation rules, bundle suggestions and a live recommendation engine.
  • Business Insightsan executive summary with stock, bundle, retention and marketing actions.
How it was built

Project workflow

The same pipeline a real analytics team would follow, scaled to a frontend project.

Study datasets

Explore the Online Retail & Instacart schemas and patterns.

Simulate data

Craft realistic sample metrics that mirror those patterns.

Design UI

Wireframe a SaaS-grade layout, palette and type system.

Build charts

Hand-code every chart in CSS and SVG — no libraries.

Add interaction

Counters, theme, search, sorting and the basket engine in vanilla JS.

Roadmap

Future enhancements

Live data ingestion

Apriori in the browser
