Business Insights

Executive summary: the simulated store generated $4.6M in annual revenue from 129,480 orders, growing 18.2% year over year. Growth is driven by returning customers (64.8% of the base), festive-season electronics, and a weekly grocery habit. The clearest opportunities are bundle offers from the basket rules, retention campaigns for the 16% at-risk segment, and stocking up on the four fastest-trending SKUs before the next peak.
